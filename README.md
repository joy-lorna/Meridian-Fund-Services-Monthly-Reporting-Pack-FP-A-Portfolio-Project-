# Meridian Monthly Management Reporting Pack

## Project Overview

This project simulates a monthly management reporting process for a fictional Luxembourg fund administration and asset-servicing business.

The objective is to demonstrate how a Financial Analyst or FP&A professional can transform operational and financial data into executive-level reporting, management insights, and decision-ready commentary.

The project combines:

- Linked Excel reporting model
- KPI scorecard framework
- Budget vs Actual analysis
- Prior-year comparisons
- Management commentary
- Executive PowerPoint reporting pack

The deliverables are designed to reflect the reporting style commonly used within fund administrators, asset servicers, and financial-services organisations operating in Luxembourg.

---

## Business Scenario

**Meridian Fund Services S.A.** is a fictional Luxembourg-based fund administration and asset-servicing organisation supporting alternative investment funds across private equity, real estate, and private credit strategies.

Senior management requires a monthly reporting pack that provides visibility into:

- Revenue performance
- Cost management
- Profitability
- Assets Under Administration (AUA)
- Operational quality
- Working-capital efficiency
- Capacity utilisation

The reporting pack is intended to support month-end performance review and management decision-making.

---

## Business Question

How can management quickly understand:

- What changed versus budget?
- What changed versus prior year?
- Which operational issues require attention?
- What actions should management take before the next reporting cycle?

## Reporting Framework

The project follows a traditional management-reporting structure:

### Actual vs Budget

Measures monthly performance against approved management targets.

### Actual vs Prior Year

Measures operational and financial improvement over time.

### Variance Analysis

Identifies:

- Revenue drivers
- Cost drivers
- Operational exceptions
- Performance risks

### Management Action Tracking

Each key issue is linked to a recommended management response.

## Key Analytical Themes

### Commercial Performance

Revenue growth driven by new fund mandates and onboarding activity.

### Operational Quality

Improving NAV error performance supported by remediation activities.

### Working Capital Management

Monitoring of overdue receivables through Days Sales Outstanding (DSO).
